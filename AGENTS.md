# AI Swiss Knife — Design System & Agent Harness Rules (`v0.2.0-spatial`)

Built on two foundational principles:
1. **Stone Librande's One-Page Design Philosophy:** Never fragment knowledge into a multi-file Wiki that hides system relationships. The entire ecosystem (Task Routes, Minard TPM Bottleneck Pipes, Barycentric Trade-Off Triangle, and Top-Down Modality Matrix) must remain visible on a single spatial page (`poster.svg` and `index.html`).
2. **Katie Dill's Scaling Intent & Craft:** Do not ship "Zombie UI" or passive documentation. Scale opinionated intent via the `ask.py` CLI harness (`python ask.py route <intent>`) so builders and agents get immediate, verified configurations at the moment of work.

## Rules for AI Agents (Antigravity)
- **Single Source of Truth:** Edit `data/core_graph.yaml` only. Never hand-edit `README.md`, `poster.svg`, or `index.html`.
- **Verify & Render:** Always run `python ask.py all` (`ingest` -> `lint` -> `render`) before committing.
- **Zero Slop Policy:** Every provider must specify `pipe_tpm` (for Minard flow scaling), `tri` `[Reasoning, Speed, Quota]` coordinates, `trains_on_data`, and `gotcha`.
